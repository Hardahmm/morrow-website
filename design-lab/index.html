Take it for a little walk.

Every concept is an app you can explore. Start anywhere.

  1. Open a collection, then a wallpaper. Tap the heart to keep it.
  2. Open Studio. Try Month, change a font and move a layer.
  3. Fit a sample image or import your own photo locally.
  4. Save your design, reopen it in Saved, then try the Apply demo.
  5. Explore Settings or the guided setup. Compare light and dark.

The export controls create real PNG and recipe files. Your Android wallpaper is never changed by this website.
