First Rep

Privacy policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated 17 November 2026

Your progress stays on your phone

First Rep stores your goals, habits, check-ins, focus sessions, ranks, alarms, selected apps and preferences locally. This release has no account, advertising, analytics, cloud sync or in-app purchase service.

Camera and movement

Optional camera missions use bundled on-device models to count movement or identify a sky photo. Frames are processed in memory, are not saved by First Rep, and are not uploaded. You can use a math mission instead. Camera access can be revoked in Android settings.

App limits and Accessibility

With your explicit consent, Focus Shield receives foreground app package names and shows a pause screen for apps you select during focus sessions or after a daily limit. It cannot retrieve screen contents and does not type, tap or change settings. Usage access supplies selected-app usage totals. You can disable either access in Android settings.

Alarms and notifications

Notification, alarm and lock-screen access enable your chosen reminders and wake missions. Android can delay background work or restrict full-screen presentation. A powered-off or force-stopped phone cannot reliably deliver an alarm.

Files and sharing

A system file picker grants access only to the audio file you select. Wallpaper application uses Android's wallpaper service. Progress sharing and feedback open a system chooser or your email app only when you request them. Information you send is then handled by the service you choose.

Retention and deletion

You can reset local progress in Settings. Clearing First Rep's app storage or uninstalling removes its local records. Applied wallpapers and files you exported can remain outside the app and can be removed separately. First Rep disables Android app-data backup.

Children and support

First Rep is a general self-improvement app. Parents and guardians should review camera, alarm and app-limit access for younger users. Exercise missions are optional and are not a medical or fitness-form assessment. For privacy or support questions, contact waytokairo@gmail.com.
