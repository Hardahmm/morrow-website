Privacy policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Morrow is an Android wallpaper and dot-calendar studio developed by the maintainer of the Hardahmm GitHub account. Its current offline app processes your wallpaper content on your device. You do not need an account to use its local tools.

This policy covers the current Morrow offline app and this Morrow website. Future versions that introduce online services, advertising or analytics will require an updated policy and any applicable disclosures or consent before those services are enabled.

What the app handles

  • Wallpaper content: images you select, imported packs, saved scenes, crop and positioning choices, fonts, colors, depth selections and rotation preferences are processed locally to create and display wallpapers.
  • Calendar and personal text: dot-calendar dates, countdowns, intentions, optional wallpaper to-do text and calendar events you choose to import are stored locally for the designs you create. Imported events may include their title, timing, calendar identifier and display color.
  • Preferences: onboarding choices, appearance, notification and refresh preferences are stored locally so the app can remember your setup.

The current offline app does not send this content to a Morrow server, operate a Morrow cloud account or upload your photos for AI processing. Included generated artwork is bundled content; daily refresh redraws your local scene or rotates saved artwork rather than generating new AI images online.

Permissions you control

  • Photos and files: Android's picker lets you choose content to import. The app uses the selected content for your wallpaper and saved scenes.
  • Calendar, optional: read access lets you import events from calendars available on your device. Morrow does not use this permission to edit your source calendars. Your calendar provider's own syncing is governed by that provider.
  • Notifications, optional: allows daily-refresh notifications.
  • Precise alarms, optional: supports scheduling refresh near midnight. Device and Android restrictions can affect delivery.
  • Wallpaper and restart handling: allows wallpaper application, live wallpaper operation and rescheduling enabled refresh after a device restart.

You can revoke permissions in Android Settings. Features that depend on a permission may stop working. Revoking calendar or photo access does not by itself remove content already imported into the app.

Sharing and third-party services

Morrow does not sell your personal data. In the current offline app, advertising, analytics and crash-reporting collection are disabled; no ad or paid-upgrade interface is offered. Some dependencies and configuration are retained for potential future functionality, but these do not mean that those services are currently active.

When you choose to export or share a wallpaper or scene, Android or the receiving app handles that action. Your output can contain your photo, tasks or event titles, depending on what you included. Check it before sharing. The receiving service's privacy policy applies to its handling of that content.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains on your device until you remove it or clear the app's storage. To remove all Morrow app data, open Android Settings, find Morrow under Apps, open its storage controls and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Names vary by device. Uninstalling also removes the app's private storage under normal Android behavior.

Files you exported to shared storage, downloads or another app can remain after you clear or uninstall Morrow. Delete those copies separately in the relevant file or receiving app. Removing an imported calendar event from Morrow does not delete it from your original calendar.

There is no Morrow cloud account to delete. If you contact the developer, information you provide will be used to answer the enquiry and retained only as needed for support or applicable obligations. Do not include sensitive calendar details or private photos in a public support request.

Security

The app uses Android's application storage protections and does not enable its own Android cloud backup. Protect your device and any exported files. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

This website and GitHub Pages

This website has no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, third-party fonts or application cookies. The homepage preview keeps its choices in page memory. The separate design-selection studio uses browser localStorage when you save favorite artwork, included-art designs, rotation selections or a theme choice. Saved designs can include text you enter. Each concept has a reset control in Settings; the selection page also lets you clear your theme choice. Clearing site data in your browser removes all of these local records.

In the design-selection studio, a personal photo is read locally for the current browser visit and is never uploaded by the site. Personal-photo designs are not persisted across visits. PNG and JSON exports create files on your device; exported files can contain your design text, so check them before sharing. Android wallpaper application and permission examples are simulations. No Android permissions are requested by this website.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses, for hosting, security and operational purposes under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following a GitHub support link takes you to a separate service governed by GitHub's policies.

Children

Morrow does not knowingly collect children's personal information through its offline tools. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer through support, contact the developer so the information can be reviewed and removed as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

The effective date will change when this policy is updated. Material changes to app data practices will also be disclosed in the app where required. This policy must be checked against the final public release and updated before enabling new data processing.

Contact the developer

For questions about Morrow, this policy or a privacy request, email waytokairo@gmail.com. Morrow is maintained by Hardahmm. For non-sensitive website issues, use the website support tracker. Public issues are visible to others; do not post private information there.
